\begin{resumo}

O trabalho analisa vulnerabilidades do OWASP Top 10 \cite{OWASP2021Top10} sob o princípio da defesa em profundidade, unificando ferramentas SAST, DAST, IAST e RASP \cite{Shahriar2011SecurityVulnerabilityTaxonomy, DeJesus2023SecurityTestingWebApps, Barth2008RobustDefensesCSRF}. A revisão bibliográfica apoiada por IA \cite{DeJesus2023SecurityTestingWebApps} revelou lacunas em \textit{shadow} APIs \cite{Escape2023GraphQLSecurity, Tanveer2025RESTfulAPISecurity} e na interpretabilidade de IA para detecção \cite{Yadav2025CuttingEdgeIDS, Alsaedi2023DeepLearningWAF}. Conclui-se pela necessidade de automação via \textit{DevSecOps} \cite{Edmundson2022SANSDevSecOps} e arquiteturas \textit{Zero Trust} \cite{NIST2020ZeroTrustArchitecture, Yadav2025ZeroTrustMicroservices}.

\noindent \textbf{Palavras-chave:} Segurança Web, Defesa em Profundidade, \textit{DevSecOps}, \textit{Zero Trust}.

\end{resumo}

\begin{abstract}

This study analyzes OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities \cite{OWASP2021Top10} through defense-in-depth, integrating SAST, DAST, IAST, and RASP \cite{Shahriar2011SecurityVulnerabilityTaxonomy, DeJesus2023SecurityTestingWebApps, Barth2008RobustDefensesCSRF}. Literature review with GenAI support \cite{DeJesus2023SecurityTestingWebApps} identified gaps in shadow API security \cite{Escape2023GraphQLSecurity, Tanveer2025RESTfulAPISecurity} and AI interpretability \cite{Yadav2025CuttingEdgeIDS, Alsaedi2023DeepLearningWAF}. Results point to \textit{DevSecOps} automation \cite{Edmundson2022SANSDevSecOps} and \textit{Zero Trust} architectures \cite{NIST2020ZeroTrustArchitecture, Yadav2025ZeroTrustMicroservices}.

\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} Web Security, Defense-in-Depth, \textit{DevSecOps}, \textit{Zero Trust}.

\end{abstract}
